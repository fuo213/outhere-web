Itinerary explorations

Three ways to make the trip panel read as one story, with hand-drawn charts built from the real Maze demo trip (4 days, 20.4 mi). Static pages; the live planner is unchanged. Rationale and build notes are in README.md next to this page.

    A hand-inked trail map of the whole route over real contours, then compact day rows.

  3. C · Field journal
